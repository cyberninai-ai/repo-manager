            How a launch works. A creator builds a ChainLaunch payload, signs it and submits it over the wire. The protocol checks it and mints the fixed supply once; mint authority is then gone. The token trades on a bonding curve (CURVE_SWAP) whose SOL stays escrowed. At 85 SOL raised the protocol signs a MIGRATE record and auto-seeds a concentrated-liquidity pool with the raised SOL and the 186.9M reserve tokens. Its seed positions belong to a lock vault that nobody, including the creator, can withdraw from. From then on the token trades on the pool (CLMM_SWAP), anyone can add or remove their own LP range (CLMM_POSITION), and the protocol publishes CLMM_POOL_STATUS.
            
    
            How a rug is ruled out. No mint after genesis, curve SOL escrowed, pool seed liquidity locked forever, and the creator's 2% unlocks only after migration, linearly over the vesting period. Every attempt to dump, withdraw or mint slashes 25% of the creator's bond to holders.
            
    
            How degen behaviour is damped. The first 60 seconds clear at one uniform price with a per-wallet cap, so sniper bots get no edge. On the curve each wallet is capped at its share of supply. Swaps are capped at 3 SOL with a 10-second wait per wallet and a slippage limit, and a 30% move within a minute halts swaps for 30 seconds. Your own guard adds per-trade, daily-loss and grade limits.
            
    
            Simulated. No chain is involved. Traders, snipers, LP bots and creators run in a Web Worker and send signed CHAIN_LAUNCH, CURVE_SWAP, CLMM_SWAP, CLMM_POSITION and MINT records, reading the protocol's status records to pick a venue. Your own records travel through the same worker wire. This page's engine verifies signatures and enforces every rule. Signatures are keyed FNV-1a checksums standing in for Ed25519; CIDs are real sha-256 CIDv1 of each token's metadata. Your wallet holds demo SOL.
